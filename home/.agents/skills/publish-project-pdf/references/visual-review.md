# Visual review

Use this checklist for a new theme or a material layout change. Render every PDF
page to a numbered PNG at a readable resolution and inspect the actual images.

## Page-level checks

- The cover establishes title, purpose, and visual identity without looking
  like an ordinary content page.
- Body headers and footers are consistent, unobtrusive, and correct.
- No page is unexpectedly blank, nearly empty, clipped, or excessively dense.
- Primary headings are not stranded at the bottom of a page. Paragraphs, lists,
  figures, and tables do not create conspicuous widows or orphans.

## Element checks

- Heading hierarchy is immediately legible. Decorative rules sit near their
  headings without touching glyphs or descenders.
- Table headers are distinct, row banding remains subtle, all cell content is
  left-aligned, and no column crosses the page margin.
- Code blocks preserve indentation and syntax colors, fit the available width,
  and remain visually associated with their surrounding explanation.
- Inline code, links, long paths, and URLs are readable and do not collide with
  adjacent text.
- Lists have consistent indentation and compact, comfortable spacing.

## Whole-document checks

- Typography, color, spacing, and repeated furniture remain consistent across
  all pages.
- The table of contents matches the visible hierarchy and page numbers.
- The final page ends deliberately rather than leaving one avoidable fragment.
- The published PDF was regenerated after the last correction and compiles
  without warnings.
